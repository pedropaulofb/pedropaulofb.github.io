## Selected publications

### Inferring ontological categories of OWL classes using foundational rules

Barcelos, P. P. F., Sales, T. P., Romanenko, E., Almeida, J. P. A., Engelberg, G., & Guizzardi, G. (2023). *Inferring ontological categories of OWL classes using foundational rules*. Formal Ontology in Information Systems — FOIS 2023. IOS Press.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3233/FAIA231122)

**Relevance:** Foundational ontology, OWL, rule-based inference, ontological categories, and Semantic Web implementation.

### A FAIR catalog of ontology-driven conceptual models

Sales, T. P., Barcelos, P. P. F., Fonseca, C. M., Souza, I. V., Romanenko, E., Bernabé, C. H., Santos, L. O. B. S., Fumagalli, M., Kritz, J., Almeida, J. P. A., & Guizzardi, G. (2023). *A FAIR catalog of ontology-driven conceptual models*. Data & Knowledge Engineering, 147, 102210.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.datak.2023.102210)

**Relevance:** FAIR model publication, OntoUML/UFO models, research infrastructure, and reusable semantic artifacts.

### Mining frequent structures in conceptual models

Fumagalli, M., Sales, T. P., Barcelos, P. P. F., Micale, G., Glaser, P.-L., Bork, D., Zaytsev, V., Calvanese, D., & Guizzardi, G. (2025). *Mining frequent structures in conceptual models*. Software and Systems Modeling.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10270-025-01295-0)

**Relevance:** Empirical analysis of conceptual models and reusable structural patterns in ontology-driven modeling.

### Ontological foundations of resilience

Barcelos, P. P. F., Calhau, R. F., Oliveira, Í., Sales, T. P., Gailly, F., Poels, G., & Guizzardi, G. (2024). *Ontological foundations of resilience*. Conceptual Modeling — ER 2024. Springer.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-75872-0_21)

**Relevance:** Foundational ontology applied to resilience, complex systems, and conceptual modeling.

### An ontology-driven domain-specific modeling language for specification and evaluation of resilience scenarios in complex systems

Barcelos, P. P. F., Calhau, R. F., Gailly, F., Poels, G., & Guizzardi, G. (2026). *An ontology-driven domain-specific modeling language for specification and evaluation of resilience scenarios in complex systems*. Enterprise Design, Operations, and Computing — EDOC 2025 Revised Selected Papers. Springer.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-15140-7_14)

**Relevance:** Ontology-driven modeling-language design, applied ontology, and evaluation of resilience scenarios.

### Enabling Semantic Traceability in Health Data: The Health-RI Semantic Interoperability Initiative

Barcelos, P. P. F., van Ulzen, N., Groeneveld, R., Konrad, A., Khalid, Q., Zhang, S., Trompert, A., & Vos, J. (2026). *Enabling Semantic Traceability in Health Data: The Health-RI Semantic Interoperability Initiative*. Accepted for SWAT4HCLS 2026.

[Accepted submissions page](https://www.swat4ls.org/workshops/amsterdam2026/programme/accepted-submissions/)

**Relevance:** Semantic traceability, ontology-based interoperability, mappings, and FAIR-aligned semantic resources in a health-data infrastructure context.

### On the importance of truly ontological distinctions for standardizations

Barcelos, P. P. F., Reginato, C. C., Monteiro, M. E., & Garcia, A. S. (2016). *On the importance of truly ontological distinctions for standardizations: A case study in the domain of telecommunications*. Computer Standards & Interfaces, 44, 28–41.

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csi.2015.09.011)

**Relevance:** Ontological analysis of technical standards, conceptual clarity, and semantic interoperability.
